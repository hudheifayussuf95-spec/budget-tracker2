How to use this tracker

Follow these simple steps:

  1. Enter the name of your expense.
  2. Enter the amount in Kenyan Shillings (KES).
  3. Select an expense category.
  4. Choose the date of the expense.
  5. Click the Add Expense button.
  6. View your expenses in the table.
